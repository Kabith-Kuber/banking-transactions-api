Full walkthrough (read this first if you're new)

Step 1 — Create accounts

Think of this like opening bank accounts for two people. Enter a name (e.g. Alice) and how much money they start with (e.g. $1,000). Click Create account.

You need at least 2 accounts before you can send money between them.

Step 2 — Send money

Pick who is sending money (From), who receives it (To), and the amount. Click Send transfer. The money is moved instantly.

Example: Alice sends $150 to Bob for rent.

Step 3 — Check balance

Select any account and click Check balance to see how much money they have right now.

Step 4 — View history

Select an account and click Load history. You'll see every payment they sent or received, with dates and notes.

Important: data only lasts while the app is running. Close or restart the server and everything resets.

Common questions

Why are my accounts gone?

The app stores data in memory. Restarting the server clears everything. Just create new accounts.

Why can't I send money?

You need two different accounts, and the sender must have enough balance for the transfer.

Do I need to copy account IDs?

No. After you create accounts, they appear in the dropdown menus automatically.
